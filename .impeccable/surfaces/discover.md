## Direction contract

THESIS: A public offering ledger for Stellar launches: inspect first, authenticate only when value moves. It refuses the noisy crypto-dashboard default.

OWN-WORLD: White paper, graphite type, hairline rules, and cobalt reserved for actions and live state. Newsreader gives launches editorial weight; Manrope keeps data exact. Lists replace card grids.

STORY: Discover a credible launch, read its terms, then use Blux to participate. Optional public and developer identities strengthen a transparent profile after authentication.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A spare masthead, one calm proposition, filters, and the first live offerings. Rows disclose format, price, progress, and timing before asking for commitment. Interaction is crisp: rule shifts, small state changes, no ornamental motion.

FORM: Editorial public-offering ledger, sixth of seven grounded forms; seed key `74c589df`.
